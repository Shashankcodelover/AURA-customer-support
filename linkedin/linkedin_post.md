# 🤖 Engineering AURA: Autonomous Customer Support & Agentic Intelligence

Scalable customer support demands more than scripted chatbots — it requires context-aware agent networks that evaluate intent, retain conversation history, and resolve complex issues autonomously. I built **AURA** to deliver intelligent, multi-agent support workflows.

### 🛠️ What I Built & Deployed:
• **Context Capsule Engine**: Contextual ticket summarization and real-time customer sentiment arc tracking that provides human agents and automated bots with complete situational awareness.
• **Agent Topology & Dispatch Network**: Visual graph representing specialized agent nodes with intelligent ticket routing and workload-balanced triage.
• **Predictive Churn Radar**: Real-time customer dissatisfaction detection with immediate automated escalation triggers and churn mitigation playbooks.
• **Enterprise Dashboard & Ingestion Pipeline**: Ingestion of multi-channel support tickets with instant knowledge-base grounding and confidence analytics.
